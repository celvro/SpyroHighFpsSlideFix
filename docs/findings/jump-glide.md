# Jump / glide / hover height

## Framerate config

- `Falcon/Config/ConsoleVariables.ini` has `[Startup] t.MaxFPS=30`
- `DefaultEngine.ini`: `bUseFixedFrameRate=True`, `FixedFrameRate=30`, `bSmoothFrameRate=False`, `SmoothedFrameRateRange` 22–62
- `Windows/WindowsEngine.ini` overrides this for PC: `bUseFixedFrameRate=False`, `FixedFrameRate=60`, `bSmoothFrameRate=True`
- `DefaultGameUserSettings.ini`: `FrameRateLimit=0`, `bUseVSync=False`

## Jump / glide framerate bug research

Symptom: Spyro reaches ledges at 30 FPS that he can't reach at 60+ (jump height and/or glide distance).

- Movement component is native `PhasmidCharacterMovementComponent` (`/Script/Phasmid`, in the exe). Spyro's blueprint (`CPS1999_Spyro/Content/Blueprints/BP_CPS1999_Playable`) doesn't override `MaxSimulationTimeStep`, so it's the engine default 0.05 and there's no substepping at 30 or 60 FPS.
- Movement values come from gameplay attributes initialized by `CharacterCommon/Content/Data/SpyroCharacterInitialDataTable` (Spyro row: `JumpZVelocity=209`, `JumpMaxHoldTime=0.233`, `GravityScale=0.77`, `GlideDescentMultiplier=-0.31`, `AirControl=1`). World gravity is -980.
- `GA_Spyro_Jump` (Blueprint): `SetupJumpEffects` applies `GE_SpyroJumpNoGravity` (GravityScale overridden to 0 for a duration equal to the `JumpMaxHoldTime` attribute) and copies `JumpMaxHoldTime` to the Character. It then runs the native `PhasmidTask_JumpWithConfirm` and calls `Character.Jump()`. Releasing the button (`OnJumpStop`) removes the no-gravity effect and calls `StopJumping` (skipped while `Character.Enable.NoJumpStop` is set).
- `GA_Spyro_Glide` only sets an initial glide velocity. Glide motion itself is native.
- Neither ability has per-tick Blueprint logic, so the framerate dependence is most likely in native movement code. The no-gravity effect's expiry is also rounded to a frame boundary.
- **Probe heights before 2026-09-13 21:10 are biased.** Old `seg` apex/landDz/horiz values were measured from the first *airborne* frame, which already rose one frame (6.97 units at 30 FPS, 1.45 at 144). That hid the jump bug. Probe and `tools/Compare-Segments.ps1` now measure from the last grounded frame.
- **Confirmed jump height bug.** Every GE_SpyroJumpNoGravity jump (ground, water, charge) peaks higher at 30 FPS. Full-hold ground or water jump: +84.64 at 30 FPS vs +79.74 at 144. Trace `build/probe/water.csv`.
  - Mechanism, from per-frame traces: with the button held to timeout, gravity stays off for ceil(H/dt) frames plus usually 1 frame of lag. That's 8 frames (0.2667 s, 55.73 units) at 30 FPS and 35 frames (0.2431 s, 50.80 units) at 144. Apex = zero-gravity rise + 209²/(2·754.6) = 28.94; that sum matches the measured apex exactly. A few jumps show 0 lag frames (7 frames at 30, 34 at 144).
  - Early release: gravity stays off exactly for the held frames (no extra frame), so the release rounds up to the frame grid.
  - Fix in `HighFpsSlidingAndJumpFix`: track the zero-gravity rise and, when the engine restores gravity, compute the 30 FPS rise: timeout → (ceil(H·30) + lag)/30, release → ceil((rise − dt/2)·30)/30. Keep `GravityScale = 0` until then, then fold the sub-frame leftover into Z velocity (vz'² = vz² + 2·g·vz·leftover) so the apex matches exactly. A replay over recorded traces showed no extension at 30 FPS and +3 frames for 144 FPS full holds. **Verified in game (2026-09-13 21:23):** full-hold standing jump apex was +84.68 at 144 FPS with the fix (×3), +84.64 at 30 FPS, and +79.74 at 144 FPS without it. Charge jump apex was +74.58 at 144 FPS with the fix vs +74.50 at 30 FPS, and 68.7–70.4 without it. The fix made no changes at 30 FPS.
- Glides: in straight-glide tests the glide phase itself (speed 367.8, descent −114) is identical at 30 and 144 FPS. Glide distance differences come from takeoff height (the jump bug above) and glide start timing. Canyon long-glide comparisons using the old biased probe numbers were inconclusive.
- **Hover at the end of a glide is affected too, and the fix covers it** (probe `rise` lines, 2026-09-14). A hover launches at vz 160 with `JumpMaxHoldTime` 0.150; `GE_SpyroHover` changes the attributes and the Character property follows. Gravity stays off 0.2000 s at 30 FPS (6 frames = ceil(0.15·30) + 1) vs 0.160 s at 144 FPS without the fix (23 frames). Apex: +48.91 at 30 FPS, **+42.5 at 144 FPS without the fix** (6.4 units lower, a bigger gap than for jumps), +48.96 at 144 FPS with it (×3 each).
- Same session: ground jumps +84.64 at 30 FPS, +84.68 with the fix, +79.7 without it. Some air/double jumps show the 0-lag variant (34 frames, +75.4), which the fix correctly leaves alone.
- Still untested: landing on ledge edges (the Steam "slips off" report may be the slide bug).
- The probe's `rise kind` classifier (Flying with average vz < −50 → glide-hover) sometimes labels a normal jump `glide-hover`. Real hovers are the ones with vz0=160 and jumpMaxHoldTime=0.150.
- The probe treats swimming as airborne, because surface swimming uses MovementMode Flying (5) like gliding. Water jumps therefore don't get their own `seg` lines; find them in the CSV as mode 5 → 3 with vz > 0.
- Never shrink `MaxSimulationTimeStep` below a frame for experiments. Substeps reproduce the high-FPS sliding bug even at 30 FPS, and the braking fix doesn't account for substeps.

**Target behaviour: every framerate must match 30 FPS** (the console tuning). Some glide distances are impossible otherwise, so a fix that makes 30 FPS behave like high FPS is wrong.


## Spyro 2 glides at 30 vs 320 FPS (2026-09-18, probe `glide` tracker, trace `trace_20260918_031820.csv`)

Setup: Spyro 2 ledge, full-hold jump + glide held straight to the ground (~12.5 s glides, ~1350 drop), some ending in a hover. Probe `glide`/`hover`/`glideair` lines (see `trackers/glide.lua`).

- **Glide Blueprint timers** (read from `GA_Spyro_Jump` / `GA_Spyro_Glide` bytecode): a glide can only start after `StartWaitForGlide` (an `AbilityTask_WaitDelay`: 0.3 s after a jump, 0.85 after a super jump, 0.75/1.5 after a hover); a request during the wait starts the glide when it ends. `SetInitialGlideVelocity`: facing · min(hspeed, 385), vz = GlideDescentMultiplier · that. A 0.4 s timer (`MinGlideThresholdForHover`) gates the hover (earlier → hover fail). Hover needs tag `Character.Enable.Hover` (Spyro 2/3) unless it's a ledge hover (GlideDuration > 100 → `GE_SpyroLedgeHover`: JumpZVelocity 80, hold 0.1, plus a 0.25 s root motion push of 200). `GE_SpyroGliding`: MaxFlySpeed 385, MaxAcceleration 850, BrakingDecelerationFlying 1000.
- **Identical at both rates:** glide start speed (185.2 h, −57.4 vz), steady glide Velocity (367.74 h, −114.0 vz), hover threshold (0.400 s), full hover apex (+48.91 at 30, +48.96 at 320 with the jump fix), jump apex (+84.64 / +84.68). A hover rise of 0.1656 s (+44.13) at 320 was an early hover-button release, which the fix rounds like 30 FPS.
- **Not tested:** the 0.3 s wait rounding. Every glide was requested 0.44–0.60 s after takeoff (after the wait), so glide start times were human timing.
- **Difference found: horizontal glide speed from position quantization.** The glide's Velocity isn't re-derived from position (it stays at exact values like 231.96), but each frame's step is rounded to the 1/32 lattice at x,y ≈ ±300000. At 320 FPS a step of 0.7249 per axis becomes 23 or 24 lattice steps every frame, the same way each frame, so the actual speed per axis is off by up to (1/32)/2·320 = 5/s, depending on heading. Measured from positions over the steady glide: **363.98 (glide 4, predicted 363.99 from rounding (223.48, 292.04)/320) and 370.74 (glide 8)** at 320 FPS vs **367.56 / 367.96** at 30 FPS (Velocity 367.74 in all). Horizontal distance per 400 units of drop: 1277.7 / 1280.4 / 1298.6 at 320 vs 1289.5–1291.1 at 30 (ideal 400/114·367.74 = 1290.3). So a glide at 320 FPS covers **−1.0% to +0.7%** of the 30 FPS distance depending on heading (about ±3 units per 100 of drop, up to ~±45 over these 1350-unit glides). Vertical descent is unaffected (z is near the origin).
- **Glide distance fix (`fixes/glide.lua`, `FIX_GLIDE_DISTANCE`), verified 2026-09-18 13:46** (trace `trace_20260918_134620.csv`): while gliding (MovementMode Flying with GE_SpyroGliding's MaxAcceleration 850 and BrakingDecelerationFlying 1000) above 30 FPS, it adds up velocity·dt minus the actual horizontal displacement and moves Spyro by whole 1/32 steps (swept `K2_AddActorWorldOffset`) once an axis reaches half a step; any frame off by more than one step (wall, script) restarts the count. Straight glides at 320 FPS: speed from positions 366.82 and 366.86 against Velocity 366.91 / 366.92 (glide 2's heading, Velocity (221.03, 293.90), would have rounded to 363.4 without it); 30 FPS 367.41 against 367.03.
- **Glide start wait lags a frame, like the no-gravity effect** (same session, jumps with the glide pressed during the wait). `StartWaitForGlide` runs on the frame after the jump's first move (0.0333 s after the last grounded frame at 30 FPS, 0.003 at 320), and the 0.3 s wait then ends exactly on time (`waited` 0.3000–0.3001 at 30, 0.3003–0.3007 at 320). So an early-pressed glide starts 0.3333 s after the jump at 30 FPS but ~0.303 s at 320: one 30 FPS frame of extra rise at 30.
  - Short hop released at 0.1 s: glide start height +49.12 at 30 FPS (matches the jump curve at t = 0.3333: 20.9 + 209·0.2333 − 377.3·0.2333² = 49.13) vs +48.26 at 320. About 1 unit, ~3 units of glide distance.
  - Full-hold jump, glide pressed during the wait: +67.99 at 30 FPS (glide 4) vs **+62.89 at 320 (glide 11, predicted 62.9)**, and the 30 FPS glide then starts ~2.5 units less far down its descent, so ~7.5 units higher at the same moment, about 24 units of glide distance.
  - A press after the wait ends starts the glide on the press frame: rounded up to the 1/30 s grid at 30 FPS (half a frame later on average), immediately at 320.
  - More 320 FPS checks against the jump curve z(t) = 209·R + 209·(t − R) − 377.3·(t − R)² (R = zero-gravity rise time, t from the last grounded frame): glide 12 (released at R = 0.2344, pressed during the wait, start 0.303) +61.67, predicted 61.55; glide 13 (full hold, pressed after the wait at 0.328, started on the press) +66.64, predicted 67.1. At 30 FPS that press would have started the glide at 0.3333 (+67.99).
- **Glide start fix, first version (position shift) broke gliding (2026-09-18).** It let the glide start early and moved Spyro to where the 30 FPS glide would be, as swept offsets over 0.1 s (logged shifts (+0.16, +0.22, +4.10), (+0.25, +0.38, +3.82), (+0.25, +0.38, +4.59)). Every such glide turned into a small hover: a standing-jump glide starts with almost no descent, so the upward offset made him rise while gliding, which the game appears to take for reaching a ledge (glide cancelled into the ledge hover: JumpZVelocity 80, hold 0.1). Lesson: never move Spyro up during a glide. Glide steering and descent are Blueprint (`CharacterInputComponent_Spyro.UpdateGlideMovement`: `AddMovementInput` of (0, 0, GlideDescentMultiplier) plus the geo-compensated forward vector), not native.
- **Glide start fix, current version (`FIX_GLIDE_START`), verified 2026-09-18.** Above 30 FPS, from the `StartWaitForGlide` hook until the glide starts or Spyro lands, it clears `GA_Spyro_Jump`'s `GlideRequested` and `WaitForGlideElapsed` every update (remembering that the wait ended and when the first press came, as a 30 FPS grid time: ceil((t − 1.5·dt − jumpStart)·30)/30), so the game can't start the glide. On the update nearest max(30 FPS wait end, 30 FPS press time) it sets `WaitForGlideElapsed` and calls `OnGlideRequested`, which starts the glide. 30 FPS wait end = jumpStart + 1/30 + ceil(wait·30)/30, jumpStart = wait hook time − its frame. Known gap: a press handled in the same frame the game's wait ends still starts the glide early (a 1-frame window).
  - Verification (320 FPS with the fix, probe hook times; a glide started by a press begins moving at the start of the frame the press is handled in, so at 30 FPS its first glide move is 1/30 s before the probe's `start`, while the fix starts it before the world tick, at the probe's `start`): early press during the wait → start +0.334 (30 FPS: +0.333), height +66.23 for a 0.2336 s rise (curve at 30 FPS: 65.9). Presses after the wait start on the 30 FPS grid: +0.412 → +0.432, +0.461 → +0.467, +0.551 → +0.566, +0.601 → +0.601, +0.342 → +0.368. Heights follow the jump curve (+82.49 at 0.467, predicted 82.44). Gliding and hovers work normally.
  - Profile with the probe also on: fixes 0.18–0.19 ms per 320 FPS frame (the probe's garbage lands in the timed region); without the probe the previous session measured 0.006–0.14 ms.
- **Glide speed-up from a standstill: timing only, not fixed (measured 2026-09-18, probe G glide test, LS122, 3 runs each).** PhysFlying moves each frame with the velocity after that frames acceleration (MaxAcceleration 850), so at 30 FPS the glide runs ahead while speeding up. The ramp runs along the glide line, so the path is the same:
  - Distance at drops of 50/100/200/400: 161.27/322.45/644.81/1289.55 at 30 FPS, 161.28–161.31/322.61–322.62/645.22–645.24/1290.47–1290.49 at 320 (runs identical to 0.03). The 0.9 gap at 400 grows with the drop: slope 3.2237 at 30 vs 3.2262 at 320 (ideal 367.74/114 = 3.2258), i.e. 30 FPSs own 1/32 position rounding on this heading (~0.1%), which the distance fix deliberately leaves alone.
  - Timing: 30 FPS reaches each drop 0.016–0.019 s sooner (d400 at 3.7233 s vs 3.7425–3.7429) and is 6.0–6.2 units further along at 1–3 s (predicted speedGained·(1/30 − 1/320)/2 = 5.8). Speed reached 50/90/99% at 0.233/0.433/0.467 s at 30 vs 0.232/0.422–0.423/0.465–0.467 at 320.
  - Not worth fixing: no height or distance a glide can reach changes, only when he gets there (~1/60 s).
  - The first G press after loading didnt start a glide (`end=mode 3`); the next ones all did.
