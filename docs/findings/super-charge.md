# Super charge (static findings 2026-09-15; measured in Wizard Peak 14:06–14:12, all fixes on)

- **Matches 30 FPS with the current fixes** (trace `trace_20260915_140642.csv`, 4 super charges at 144 FPS, 6 at 30; tag detection worked, no errors):
  - Stage times after stage 0: stage 1 +0.751 s at 144 vs +0.767 at 30 (WaitDelay rounds up to a frame), stage 2 +1.000 at both, stage 3 +4.00. From stage 0 to stage 3: 5.758 vs 5.768 s.
  - Speed ramp from a 458.5 charge: t550/t650/t745 = 0.617–0.623/1.286–1.293/1.923–1.949 s at 144 and 0.643–0.659/1.310–1.334/1.957–1.985 at 30. Both are exactly one frame behind the ideal 150/s ramp (0.610/1.277/1.910), because the timing origin is the frame before the first tagged frame. Grounded `accelObserved` is 146–150 at both. Stage 3 reaches 1025 at 144 on the ideal schedule (t850/t950/t1020 = ideal to the ms).
  - Full-lock turns at 750: yaw rate 130.7–130.8 at both, slip +0.86° at both (model 0.86). The slip fix writes GroundFriction 73.6 at 144. Without it the model gives ~4.3° at 144.
  - Camera: same distance, height, pitch and FOV (80) per stage. m_ctrInterp is 3.856 at 144 (fix) vs 3.5. Short turns trail 28.9° at 144 vs 27.0–29.5° at 30.
  - Ramp assist jumps (vz0 400): apex **+226.01 at both** (5 jumps). Gravity is off 0.2917 s at 144 vs 0.2667 at 30; the jump fix's leftover fold-in makes the apex exact anyway.
  - The ramp that launches you far away (GravityScale 0.77 for ~8 s with Flying/None modes, position jumps ~378k) can't be compared.
- **"Skitter / short hop back into the ground" at a ramp end is the charge crash** (`AM_CPS1999_Charge_Crash`): horizontal speed drops to ~0 in one frame, then Spyro is knocked up and back at vz ~187–197, speed 100. It happened at both framerates against the same wall: at 144 FPS airborne after a tapped jump off the ramp (x 1779, y −299036, z 1058), and at 30 FPS grounded (x 1798, y −299023, z 1037). There were two more crashes at 30 elsewhere. Not framerate-specific in this data.
- **Ramp-end "skitter" follow-up (144 FPS full ramp run, 14:12, super charge 11 / seg 23):** no crash and no airborne frame before takeoff. The launch was normal (vz0 400, apex +226.01), then a glide (367.7/−114) and a clean landing. The hop was lower than the best one because of where the jump started. The best hop (t 270.5) left from the flat top (y −302198, z 682.5, floor nz 0.999, speed 920) and peaked at z 908.6. This one left 155 units earlier on the slope (y −302043, z 640.6) and peaked at z 866.6. The Wizard Peak ramp is faceted (floor nz 0.8944 → 0.9363 → 0.9506/0.9375 seam at y≈−302035 → 0.9641 → 0.9506 → 0.9992 top from y≈−302166). The seam's floor-normal/floor-distance blip was also crossed on the best run, so the 1–2 frame "skitter" is most likely that seam and not a framerate effect. The 30 FPS ramp jumps took off earlier still (y −301929 to −301997).
- Reaching stage 3 top speed needs ~5.8 s of super charge plus ~1.8 s more of uninterrupted acceleration (150/s from 750 to 1025) at both framerates.

Static findings:

- Driven by `GA_Spyro_Charge` once `Character.Enable.SuperCharge` is on (`BP_SuperChargeVolume`, gates). It applies `GE_SpyroSuperCharging` (tag `Character.MoveState.SuperCharging`, `SuperChargeLevel` 0), then `GE_SpyroSuperChargeStageOne/Two/Three` (child tags `.StageOne/.StageTwo/.StageThree`, levels 1–3) from `AbilityTask_WaitDelay` timers (0.75 s and 1 s delays seen; the rest of the chain is unread). `Character.Enable.SuperCharge.Alternate` gives `StageAlt` (level −1) instead.
- `GA_Spyro_MovementController` swaps the movement effect by stage tag. **S0–S2: MaxWalkSpeed 750, MaxAcceleration 150, GroundFriction 25, RotationInterpSpeed 6, RotationRateYaw 720. S3: MaxWalkSpeed 1025, otherwise the same. Alt: 850 / 500 / friction 12 / RotationInterpSpeed 8.** At 144 FPS a frame's 150·dt is 1.04, well under the 4.5 velocity quantization step, so acceleration there depends on the walking velocity fix.
- The Charging tag seems to stay on (the dust Blueprint checks IsCharging and not SuperCharging), so the charge fixes probably apply. Slip fix at friction 25 and MaxAcceleration 150 would write ~73 at 144 FPS (unverified).
- Jumps: `GE_Spyro_Movement_SuperChargeJumping` (JumpZVelocity 300, JumpMaxHoldTime 0.2, GravityScale 0.8, FallingLateralFriction 12, RotationInterpSpeed 8). On ramps, `BP_SuperChargeJumpAssist` (re-applies `GE_SpyroSuperChargeJumpAssistEnabled` every tick while overlapping) passes GravityScale 0.5, JumpZVelocity 400, JumpMaxHoldTime 0.25 (class defaults; instances may override) to `GE_Spyro_Movement_SuperChargeJumpingMax` via SetByCaller. `GE_SpyroSuperChargeRampFail` sets GravityScale 0.1 for a duration.
- Camera: the same charge `FollowCameraSettings` push as a normal charge (m_ctrInterp 3.5, m_radDefault 375, `Vector(10, 1, 500)`).
- Probe: `supercharge` lines (stage times, `t<speed>` next to the ideal 150/s ramp, air time), a `supercharge N stage S` line per stage (speed, accelObserved, friction, camera distance/height/pitch/FOV/offset, m_ctrInterp, m_radDefault), a `seg N supercharge` line per airborne segment (takeoff speed, jump attributes, gravity scale profile), and `turn` lines now print `super`, `slipModel30`, `slipModelHere` and `camLagModelHere`. New CSV columns: `sc, sc_stage, max_accel, jump_z_velocity, falling_lateral_friction, cam_dist, cam_height, cam_fov, cam_rad_default`. Stage detection prefers `ASC:HasMatchingGameplayTag` from Lua (untested; the `detectedBy` field shows whether it worked). If that fails it falls back on the movement attributes, which can't tell stages 0–2 apart.
