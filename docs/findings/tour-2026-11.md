# What the first full trilogy tour found (2026-11-04/05)

The world tour's first complete run over all three games. 1843 stops, each played four times — 30, 30, 60
and 160 FPS — in **1012 minutes with 12 restarts**, driven by `tools/Tour-Unattended.ps1`. 278569
position samples, 497795 animation samples. Everything is in `build/tour/20261104_194938/`; read
`compare-autotest.txt` and `compare-anims.txt`, not the logs.

How a finding is told from noise, and why both tests are needed, is in `docs/probe.md`. The short version:
the second 30 FPS pass is the stop's own noise floor, and a finding needs **60 and 160 to be wrong in the
same way**, because a framerate cause shows at both while a stop that merely wandered goes somewhere
different each time.

## The result

**37 cross-checked findings** out of 528 stops measured at more than one framerate, plus 30 more seen at
only one framerate (weaker — nothing to cross-check them against). For scale, the same data yields 426
"findings" if the floor is used on its own and 1108 stops "drift more than 10 units".

By script, over all 68 stops that cleared the floor and the agreement test:

| charge | 20 | **every one of the 16 findings over 200 units** |

By game: 37 in Spyro 1, 4 in Spyro 2, 27 in Spyro 3 — but the games are not equally covered. Only game 1
finished all four passes on nearly all its stops (~460 of 478). Game 2 measured 227 stops at 30 FPS and
87 at 160; game 3, 535 against 168. The crashes and stalls that cost those passes are all in
`driver.log`, in LS211/LS212 and LS30x/LS32x.

## Charge is the finding

Every one of the 16 findings over 200 units is a charge, or a walk that ends in one. The biggest, with
the stop's own floor beside them (all cross-checked at 60 **and** 160):

`BP_CES1060_Cupid_C` is the one to open first. It is a finding in **two different levels** (LS126/361 and
LS127/369) with the same shape, and at LS126 the 30 FPS pass moved only **54.8 units** while both fast
passes went over 750 — so this is not a charge drifting further, it is a charge that barely happens at 30
FPS and runs away at speed. `BP_SnowLeopard_C` is a finding at both its stops (walk 687 and charge 689),
and `CES1053_Strongarms_C` at three (314 walk, 315 flame, 319 charge).

Three stops disagree in **both** position and animation at high framerate, which is the strongest
pointer this run produced: **LS211/689 charge, LS322/1556 charge, LS323/1606 charge**.

This is a direction, not a cause. Charge slip is already fixed (`docs/findings/charge-turn-camera.md`),
as is the wall stall (`docs/findings/charge-wall-stall.md`); none of those stops is in a level the fixes
were verified in, and the tour runs with the shipped mod loaded, so these are what is **left** after it.

## What the run showed about the tour itself

Two artefacts were larger than anything in the game, and both are now handled. They are written up in
`docs/probe.md`, and briefly:

- **9% of runs started before the teleport landed** (349 of 3733, 182 stops): the first second of samples
  was taken 3000-odd units away, with the camera unplaced. It depends on the framerate — 11% of the 30
  FPS runs, 7% of the 160 FPS ones — so it reads as a framerate difference, and it was **17 of the 21
  stops in the first headline table**. `play.settle` now waits to actually be on the spot;
  `Compare-Autotest.ps1` discards such runs from data already recorded.
- **The 10–50 unit band is two frames of walking.** Walking measured 269 units/s, so a 30 FPS frame is 9
  units, and 44 of the 68 findings clustered around 16. The script's press and release land on a frame
  boundary; the bar is now two frames, measured from the run.

## Not findings, but worth a look

- **199 stops measured nothing**: the 30 FPS pass itself moved less than 10 units, so the stop never
  played. Mostly `flame` stops, where that is expected (a flame does not move him, and
  `Compare-Anims.ps1` is what measures those), but the `walk` ones among them are either a spot against a
  wall or the game holding the input, and `compare-autotest.txt` names them.
- **11 stops have no floor** — the repeat pass never played them, so they are unmeasured rather than
  clean.
- **93 stops drift past their floor but their fast passes disagree with each other.** Charge dominates
  here too. Not framerate findings by this test, but a stop whose outcome is that unstable at a fixed
  framerate is its own small problem.
- `AM_CES1062_HauntedTinSoldier_Asleep` (LS322/385 flame) advanced 0.0688 per sample at 30 FPS, **0 at
  30 on the repeat pass and 0.001 at 160** — a montage that essentially stops advancing. The montage
  sweep's own table is at the end of `compare-anims.txt`.
